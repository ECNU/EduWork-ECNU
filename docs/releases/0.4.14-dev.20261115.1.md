# EduWork@ECNU 0.4.14-dev.20261115.1

## 更新内容

- 新增华东师范大学 2026–2027 学年校历。周视图和月视图支持查看教学周、单双周、考试周及寒暑假，同时展示本科生和研究生报到等校级事项。支持导入个人日程和管理多个日历订阅。[学校校历](https://bksy.ecnu.edu.cn/b6/32/c40631a767538/page.htm)
- 邮件助手可按用户设置定时检查新邮件，以只读方式提取发件人、主题和正文片段。桌宠支持显示插件通知。[邮件助手](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/187) · [桌宠通知](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/267)
- 桌面端基于 DeepSeek Harness 0.2.2-rc.3。Windows 启动等待超过 30 秒时可看到等待时长和日志目录。客户端运行期间会定期检查软件更新，电脑唤醒或恢复网络后也会补查。[运行时](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/309) · [启动提示](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/241) · [软件更新](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/310)
- 远程访问升级到 0.1.7，包含 iPadOS 16 远控页面白屏修复和思考翻译兼容改进。[移动端修复](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/312) · [思考翻译](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/311)

## What's new

- Browse the official 2026–2027 ECNU academic calendar in week and month views, including teaching weeks, odd and even weeks, exams, breaks, and undergraduate and graduate arrivals. Import personal events and manage multiple calendar subscriptions.
- Enable periodic read-only mail checks to see the sender, subject and a short excerpt. The desktop pet can display plugin notifications.
- The desktop app now uses DeepSeek Harness 0.2.2-rc.3. On Windows, the startup screen shows elapsed time and the log location after 30 seconds. The app also checks for updates while running and after wake or network reconnection.
- Remote Access 0.1.7 includes a fix for blank remote pages on iPadOS 16 and improves compatibility with reasoning translation.
