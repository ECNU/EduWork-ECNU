EduWork@ECNU 0.4.10

[中文](#cn-0410) | [English](#en-0410)

<h2 id="cn-0410">中文</h2>

本次提供 **Windows x64**、**macOS Apple Silicon（arm64）** 与 **macOS Intel（x64）** 桌面包，基于公版 **EduWork 0.4.10**，内核升级至 **DSH 0.2.1-rc.5**。

- **侧边聊天**：从消息按钮或选中文字后的操作栏开启侧聊，带着原会话的历史快照在右侧继续提问，多条批注可以一起发送。侧聊与主会话分别进行，关闭后停止生成并归档，可从归档会话恢复为普通会话（公版 [#210](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/210)、[#253](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/253)）。
- **学校决策工具**：复用学校登录调用 `ecnu-decision`，支持按标签分类、判断条件成立概率和按量表评分。工具与配套技能由公版独立插件提供，学校版配置机构服务与授权，并补齐新安装和旧配置升级兼容（学校 [#73](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork-ECNU/pull/73)，公版 [#257](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/257)、[#269](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/269)）。
- **音乐服务接入准备**：配齐学校授权、异步音乐任务、产物下载和乐谱工具，为音乐模型正式开放后的技能接入提供基础（学校 [#70](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork-ECNU/pull/70)）。
- **启动与恢复**：复用官方加载页展示启动等待状态，改进退出恢复；macOS 启动准备期间隐藏应用后保持后台运行。Windows 更新后的启动健康标记支持短暂文件占用重试；Host 异常退出时区分外部终止与原生崩溃，改善错误提示（公版 [#209](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/209)、[#239](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/239)、[#247](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/247)、[#249](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/249)）。
- **Office 与桌宠**：更新 Office 技能的文件验证指引，使用校验工具和 LibreOffice Kit；修正朝右绘制的桌宠拖动、探头动画方向（公版 [#258](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/258)、[#259](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/259)）。
- **内核与许可**：升级 [DSH 0.2.1-rc.5](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/releases/tag/dsh-v0.2.1-rc.5)，采用改进后的思考内容展示，并纳入 rc.4 的 ACP 输出修复。项目主许可证迁移至木兰 PSL v2，保留历史 MIT 授权及第三方许可声明（学校 [#72](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork-ECNU/pull/72)，公版 [#256](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/256)、[#263](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/263)）。

<h2 id="en-0410">English</h2>

Desktop packages are available for **Windows x64**, **macOS Apple Silicon (arm64)** and **macOS Intel (x64)**, based on **EduWork 0.4.10** with **DSH 0.2.1-rc.5**.

- **Side chat:** Open a side conversation from a message button or text selection, bringing a snapshot of the source history into the right sidebar. Send multiple annotations together. Side conversations proceed separately from the main chat; closing stops generation and archives the conversation, which can later be restored as a regular chat (public [#210](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/210), [#253](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/253)).
- **School decision tools:** Use school sign-in to call `ecnu-decision` for classification, condition probabilities and rubric scoring. The standalone public plugin supplies the tool and skill; the school edition configures its service and authorization, including compatibility for new installations and existing configurations (school [#73](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork-ECNU/pull/73), public [#257](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/257), [#269](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/269)).
- **Music service preparation:** Prepare school authorization, asynchronous music tasks, artifact downloads and notation tools for skill integration when the music model launches (school [#70](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork-ECNU/pull/70)).
- **Startup and recovery:** Use the upstream loading page for startup status and improve quit recovery. Keep macOS in the background when hidden during startup. Retry transient Windows startup-health file locks after updates. Distinguish external Host termination from native crashes for clearer error reporting (public [#209](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/209), [#239](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/239), [#247](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/247), [#249](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/249)).
- **Office and desktop pets:** Update Office skill guidance to use validators and LibreOffice Kit. Correct drag and peek direction for pet artwork drawn facing right (public [#258](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/258), [#259](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/259)).
- **Runtime and licensing:** Upgrade to [DSH 0.2.1-rc.5](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/releases/tag/dsh-v0.2.1-rc.5) for improved reasoning display, also including the rc.4 ACP output fix. Adopt Mulan PSL v2 as the project license while retaining historical MIT grants and third-party notices (school [#72](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork-ECNU/pull/72), public [#256](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/256), [#263](https://github.com/ECNU/EduWork/pull/263)).
